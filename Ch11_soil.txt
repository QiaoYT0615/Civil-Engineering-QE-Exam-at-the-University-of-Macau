[m]公式: 
(1)Use σ1 and σ3 to get phimax:
sinphimax=(σ1-σ3)/(σ1+σ3+2c*ctgphi)
where phimax＜phi (safe state), phimax=phi (critical state - failed), phimax＞phi (impossible state - failed).
(2)Use σ3 to get σ_1f:
σ_1f=σ_3*tan^2(45°+phi/2)+2c*tan(45°+phi/2)
where σ_1＜σ_1f (safe state), σ_1=σ_1f (critical state - failed).
(3)Use σ1 to get σ_3f:
σ_3f=σ_1*tan^2(45°-phi/2)+2c*tan(45°-phi/2)
where σ_3＞σ_1f (safe state), σ_1=σ_1f (critical state - failed).
(4) Typical values of friction angles in sand:
Sand, Rounded grains, loose: phi'=27°-30°
Sand, Angular grains, dense: phi'=40°-45°
Gravel with some sand: phi'=34°-48°
Silts: phi'=26°-35°
