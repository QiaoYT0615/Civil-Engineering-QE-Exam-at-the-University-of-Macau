[m]Q9-16. A certain compressible layer has a thickness of 3.8 m. After 1.5 years, when the clay is 50% consolidated, 7.3 cm of settlement has occurred. For a similar clay and loading conditions, how much settlement would occur at the end of 1.5 years and 5 years if the thickness of this new layer were 38 m?
[m]思路:Assume both are double drainage,
Case 1: H=3.8 m, n=2, Hdr=H/n ; t=1.5yr (u=50%), St=7.3cm
St=Sc*Uavg --- Sc=?
u=50% ＜60% --- T=(pai/4)*u^2 --- T=?
T=(Cv*t)/Hdr^2 --- Cv=?
Case 2: H=38 m, n=2, Hdr=H/n ; t=1.5yr (St=?) ; t=5yr (St=?)
the same Cv as case 1,
T -- u --- If we know Sc [Sc2=Sc1*H2/H1] --- St
提示: 一维固结沉降，同样的粘土质地 --- 压缩指数Cc，初始孔隙比e0，单位重等都一样。 
同样的荷载条件，应力变化Δσ'一致。
唯一变化是涂层厚度H。
Sc=H*(Cc/(1+e0))*log((σv0'+Δσv)/σv0') --- Sc与H成正比
Sc2/Sc1=H2/H1 --- Sc2=Sc1*H2/H1
[m]Solution
Given Data:
- Initial layer thickness: H = 3.8 m
- Time elapsed: t = 1.5 yr
- Observed settlement: s(t) = 7.3 cm
- Degree of consolidation: U = 50%
- Time factor for U = 50%: T = 0.197
- Double drainage condition assumed
Step 1: Compute the Coefficient of Consolidation (cv)
T = (cv * t) / Hdr^2
Since double drainage is assumed,
Hdr = H / 2 = 3.8 / 2 = 1.9 m
cv = (T * Hdr^2) / t = (0.197 * 1.9^2) / 1.5 = 0.474 m^2/yr
Step 2: Compute the Final Settlement (sc)
sc = s(t) / U = 7.3 / 0.5 = 14.6 cm
(a) Settlement for New Layer at t = 1.5 years, H = 38 m
For new layer:
H = 38 m, Hdr = 38 / 2 = 19 m
T = (cv * t) / Hdr^2 = (0.474 * 1.5) / (19^2) = 0.0019
From consolidation tables, for T = 0.0019, we get U    5.0%.
s(t) = sc * U = (1.46 m) * (0.05) = 0.073 m = 7.3 cm
(b) Settlement for New Layer at t = 5 years, H = 38 m
T = (cv * t) / Hdr^2 = (0.474 * 5) / (19^2) = 0.00656
From consolidation tables, for T = 0.00656, we get U    9.14%.
s(t) = sc * U = (146 cm) * (0.0914) = 13.3 cm
Final Answer:
- Settlement after 1.5 years: 7.3 cm
- Settlement after 5 years: 13.3 cm

[m]Q9-18. A layer of normally consolidated clay 4.2 m thick has an average void ratio of 1.1. Its compression index is 0.52 and its coefficient of consolidation is 0.8 m^2/yr. When the existing vertical pressure on the clay layer is doubled, what change in thickness of the clay layer will result? 
[m]SOLUTION: 
Given: 
C_c = 0.52, e_o = 1.1
H = 4.2 m, σ_o = σ_p (initial vertical pressure), Δσ = σ_p (new vertical pressure) 
Use Eq. 8.18b: s_c = C_c *[ H_o / (1 + e_o)] * log[(σ'_v0 + Δσ_v) / σ'_p)]
s_c = (0.52) * [(4.2 m) / (1 + 1.1)] * log(2σ'_p/σ'_p)
s_c = (1.04) * (0.301) 
s_c = 0.313 m = 313 mm
Answer: The change in thickness of the clay layer will be 313 mm.
