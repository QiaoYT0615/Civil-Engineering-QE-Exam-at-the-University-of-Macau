2.碰撞率计算(事故率/严重度)

[m]Q5-2 A local jurisdiction has determined that for a given set of geometric conditions, a maximum rate of eight crashes/million entering vehicles can be tolerated. At an intersection of two roadways with ADTs of 10,000 and 7500, how many crashes can occur before corrective action must be sought?
5-2 一个地方管辖区确定，在特定的几何条件下，每百万进入车辆的最大碰撞率为8起。在两个道路交叉口的ADT分别为10,000和7500时，最多允许发生多少次碰撞，超过该数量则需采取纠正措施？
公式: 多用于intersections交叉口的安全性评估: RMEV (每百万进入车辆的碰撞次数)
RMEV=(A*10^6)/(ADT_total*365)
A是每年碰撞总数

Known conditions:
Maximum crash rate: 8 crashes per million entering vehicles
ADT for first road: 10,000 vehicles/day
ADT for second road: 7,500 vehicles/day

Steps and equations:
Calculate total ADT at intersection:
ADT_total = 10,000 + 7,500 = 17,500 vehicles/day
Use the formula for rate of million entering vehicles (RMEV):
RMEV = A × (1,000,000) ÷ (ADT × 365 days)
Solve for A (maximum allowable crashes per year):
8 = A × (1,000,000) ÷ (17,500 × 365)
A = 8 × 17,500 × 365 ÷ 1,000,000
A = 51.1 crashes
Round to 51 crashes per year as the maximum allowable before corrective action must be sought.

[m]Q5-3 Studies were conducted at two sites on rural roads with similar characteristics. The first site was 5.1 miles in length with an ADT of 6500. Over the year-long study period, 28 crashes occurred on this portion of roadway, five of them resulting in fatalities. The second site was a 10-mile section with an ADT of 5000. There were 32 crashes in this section with four fatalities. Determine the appropriate crash rates for both locations, and discuss the implications.
5-3 研究在具有相似特征的两处乡村道路上进行。第一个地点长度为5.1英里，ADT为6500。在为期一年的研究期间，该道路上发生了28起碰撞，其中5起导致死亡。第二个地点是一段10英里的道路，ADT为5000，该段道路发生了32起碰撞，其中4起导致死亡。确定这两个地点的适当碰撞率，并讨论其影响。
公式: 多用于road segments路段的安全性评估: RMVM (每一亿车英里行驶的碰撞次数)
RMVM=(A*10^8)/(ADT_total*365*L)
L是路段长度
致命事故率通过比例计算: RMVM_total*(致命事故数/总事故数)

Known conditions:
Site 1: 5.1 miles length, ADT = 6,500, 28 total crashes (5 fatal) in 1 year
Site 2: 10 miles length, ADT = 5,000, 32 total crashes (4 fatal) in 1 year

Steps and equations:
Calculate crash rate per 100 million vehicle-miles traveled (RMVMT) for Site 1:
RMVMT = A × (100,000,000) ÷ (ADT × 365 days × length of road section)
RMVMT_1 = 28 × (100,000,000) ÷ (6,500 × 365 × 5.1)
RMVMT_1 = 231.4 crashes per 100 million VMT
Calculate fatal crash rate for Site 1:
RMVMT_F1 = RMVMT_1 × (# of fatal ÷ # of total)
RMVMT_F1 = 231.4 × (5 ÷ 28)
RMVMT_F1 = 41.3 fatal crashes per 100 million VMT
Calculate crash rate for Site 2:
RMVMT_2 = 32 × (100,000,000) ÷ (5,000 × 365 × 10.0)
RMVMT_2 = 175.3 crashes per 100 million VMT
Calculate fatal crash rate for Site 2:
RMVMT_F2 = RMVMT_2 × (# of fatal ÷ # of total)
RMVMT_F2 = 175.3 × (4 ÷ 32)
RMVMT_F2 = 21.9 fatal crashes per 100 million VMT

Although site 2 experiences more crashes, with a lower ADT, over the one year period, the crash rate for that site is lower than for site 1.
This is due to the fact that the roadway section is almost twice as long for site 2, increasing the total vehicle miles traveled and thereby reducing the rate per 100 million vehicle miles traveled.
Site 1 can be consider less safe than site 2 based on both crash rate and fatal crash rate.

[m]Q5-6 Determine the severity number for a section of highway that had 2 fatal crashes, 24 personal injury crashes, and 74 property damage-only crashes during a particular time period. Use a weighting scheme of 20 for fatal crashes, 5 for personal injury crashes, and 1 for property damage-only crashes. Determine the severity index by dividing the severity number by the total number of crashes, thereby normalizing the severity number by the number of crashes.
5-6 计算某段高速公路的严重程度数，该段在特定时间段内发生了2起致命碰撞、24起人身伤害碰撞和74起仅财产损失碰撞。使用致命碰撞权重20、人身伤害碰撞权重5和仅财产损失碰撞权重1的加权方案。通过将严重程度数除以总碰撞数，得到严重程度指数，从而标准化严重程度数。

Known conditions:
2 fatal crashes, weighting factor = 20
24 personal injury crashes, weighting factor = 5
74 property damage-only crashes, weighting factor = 1

Steps and equations:
Calculate severity number (SN):
SN = (Fatal crashes × 20) + (Injury crashes × 5) + (PDO crashes × 1)
SN = (2 × 20) + (24 × 5) + (74 × 1)
SN = 40 + 120 + 74 = 234
Calculate severity index:
SI = SN ÷ Total number of crashes
SI = 234 ÷ (2 + 24 + 74)
SI = 234 ÷ 100 = 2.34

4.危险地点识别(危险度评估)

[m]Q5-12 A rural primary road segment of 2 miles long has an AADT of 11,350. The number of crashes that have occurred over the past 5 years are 5 fatal, 55 injury crashes, and 100 property damage-only crashes. Statewide average crashes for this type of road are 2 fatal, 130 injury, and 300 property damage-only crashes per 100 MVMT. The weighting factors for fatal, injury, and property crashes are 8, 3, 1, respectively. Using the critical crash ratio methodology, determine whether this site can be labeled a hazardous site. Use a 95% confidence limit.
5-12 一段长2英里的乡村主干道的年平均每日交通量（AADT）为11,350。在过去的5年里，该段道路发生了5起致命碰撞、55起伤害碰撞和100起仅财产损失碰撞。对于该类型道路的全州平均碰撞数为每1亿VMT发生2起致命、130起伤害和300起仅财产损失碰撞。致命、伤害和财产损失碰撞的权重因子分别为8、3、1。使用关键碰撞比率方法，确定该地点是否可以被标记为危险地点。使用95%的置信限。
公式: 交通基数 MEV_i=(Years*AADT*Segment Length*365)/(100*10^6)
3年期平均等效PDO事故率 AVR=用致命事故、伤害事故和财产损失事故，分别乘以权重并求和。
关键事故率: CR=AVR+0.5/TB+TF*(AVR/TB)^0.5 [TB=MEV_i, TF=1.96]
路段实际历史等效事故数: Segment crash history/TB=每100MVMT的等效事故
事故比率crash ratio=等效事故/CR

Known conditions:
Rural primary road segment: 2 miles long, AADT = 11,350
5-year crash data: 5 fatal, 55 injury, 100 PDO crashes
Statewide averages per 100 MVMT: 2 fatal, 130 injury, 300 PDO crashes
Weight factors: fatal = 8, injury = 3, PDO = 1
Confidence level: 95%

Steps and equations:
(1) Calculate traffic base (MEV_i):
MEV_i = Years × AADT × Length × 365 ÷ (100 × 10^6)
MEV_i = 5 × 11,350 × 2.0 × 365 ÷ (100 × 10^6)
MEV_i = 0.414 × 100 MVMT
(2) Calculate average equivalent PDO crash rate (AVR):
AVR = 8 × 2 + 3 × 130 + 300 = 706 equivalent PDO crashes per 100 MVMT
(3) Select test factor based on confidence level (95%): TF = 1.96
(4) Compute critical rate (CR):
CR = AVR + (0.5 ÷ MEV_i) + TF × √(AVR ÷ MEV_i)
CR = 706 + (0.5 ÷ 0.414) + 1.96 × √(706 ÷ 0.414)
CR = 706 + 1.207 + 80.9 = 788 equivalent PDO crashes per 100 MVMT
(5) Calculate site crash history in equivalent PDO crashes:
Site crash history = 8 × 5 + 3 × 55 + 100 = 40 + 165 + 100 = 305
(6) Calculate equivalent crash rate:
Equivalent rate = 305 ÷ 0.414 = 737 equivalent PDO crashes per 100 MVMT
(7) Calculate crash ratio:
Crash ratio = 737 ÷ 788 = 0.935
Since ratio < 1, site is not considered hazardous

[m]Q5-8 The crash rate on a heavily traveled two-lane rural highway is abnormally high. The corridor is 14 miles long with an ADT of 34,000. An investigation has determined that head-on collisions are most common, with an RMVMT of 4.5 and are caused by vehicles attempting to pass. Determine an appropriate countermeasure and calculate the estimated yearly reduction in total crashes.
5-8 在乡村双车道公路上的碰撞率异常高。该走廊长14英里，ADT为34,000。调查显示，正面碰撞最常见，RMVMT为4.5，其原因是车辆尝试超车。确定一个适当的对策，并计算估计的年碰撞总数减少量。

Known conditions:
Road length: 14 miles
ADT: 34,000 vehicles/day
RMVMT for head-on collisions: 4.5
Countermeasure: Adding passing lanes with crash reduction factor of 0.25

Steps and equations:
(1) Calculate annual crashes using RMVMT formula:
RMVMT = A × 100,000,000 ÷ (ADT × 365 × length)
4.5 = A × 100,000,000 ÷ (34,000 × 365 × 14)
A = 4.5 × 34,000 × 365 × 14 ÷ 100,000,000
A = 7.82 ≈ 8 crashes per year
(2) Calculate crash reduction:
Crash reduction = Annual crashes × Crash reduction factor
Crash reduction = 8 × 0.25 = 2 crashes per year

The number of crashes occurring at the intersection during the past three years were 28, 30, and 31, and the AADTs during those years were 8450, 9150, and 9850, respectively. Determine the expected reductions in the number of crashes during the first three years after the implementation of the countermeasures if the AADT during the first year of implementation is 10,850 and the estimated traffic growth rate is 4% per annum after implementation.
5-20 一名工程师提出在一个交叉口实施四项对策以减少高碰撞率。这些对策的CRFs分别为0.25、0.30、0.17和0.28。在过去三年中该交叉口的碰撞数分别为28、30和31，AADTs分别为8450、9150和9850。计算实施对策后的前三年，预计的碰撞减少量，如果实施第一年ADT为10,850，并且实施后预计交通增长率为每年4%。

Known conditions:
Four countermeasures with CRFs: 0.25, 0.30, 0.17, 0.28
Past three years crashes: 28, 30, 31
Past three years AADT: 8,450, 9,150, 9,850
First year of implementation AADT: 10,850
Traffic growth rate: 4% per annum

Steps and equations:
(1) Calculate combined crash reduction factor (CR):
CR = CR1 + (1-CR1)CR2 + (1-CR1)(1-CR2)CR3 + (1-CR1)(1-CR2)(1-CR3)CR4
CR = 0.25 + (1-0.25)0.30 + (1-0.25)(1-0.30)0.17 + (1-0.25)(1-0.30)(1-0.17)0.28

(2) Calculate average annual crashes:
Average crashes = (28 + 30 + 31) ÷ 3 = 29.67
(3) Calculate average past AADT:
Average past AADT = (8,450 + 9,150 + 9,850) ÷ 3 = 9,150
(4) Calculate Year 1 crash reduction:
Y1 reduction = 29.67 × 0.69 × (10,850 ÷ 9,150) = 24.3 crashes
(5) Calculate Year 2 crash reduction (with 4% growth):
Y2 reduction = 29.67 × 0.69 × (10,850 × 1.04 ÷ 9,150) = 25.3 crashes
(6) Calculate Year 3 crash reduction (with additional 4% growth):
Y3 reduction = 29.67 × 0.69 × (10,850 × 1.04^2 ÷ 9,150) = 26.3 crashes
