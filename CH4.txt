[m]4-3 Speed data collected on an urban roadway yielded a standard deviation in speeds of ±4.8 mi/h.
(a) If an engineer wishes to estimate the average speed on the roadway at a 95% confidence level so that the estimate is within 2 mi/h of the true average, how many spot speeds should be collected?
(b) If the estimate of the average must be within ±1 mi/h, what should the sample size be?
4-3 在城市道路上收集的速度数据显示标准差为±4.8 mi/h。
(a) 如果一名工程师希望在95%置信水平下估计道路的平均速度，并且估计结果在真平均值±2 mi/h的范围内，那么应收集多少个点速度？
(b) 如果估计的平均值需在±1 mi/h的范围内，所需的样本量是多少？
Known Conditions:
Standard deviation of speed data: ±4.8 mi/h
Confidence level: 95% (z = 1.96)
Desired tolerance in part (a): ±2 mi/h
Desired tolerance in part (b): ±1 mi/h
Formulation:
N = [(z·σ)/d]^2
Steps:
For part (a) with tolerance d = 2 mi/h:
N = [1.96(4.8)/2]^2 = 22.1 ≈ 23 spot speeds
For part (b) with tolerance d = 1 mi/h:
N = [1.96(4.8)/1]^2 = 88.5 ≈ 89 spot speeds

[m]4-4 An engineer wishing to obtain the speed characteristics on a bypass around her city at a confidence level of 95% and an acceptable limit of ±1.0 mi/h collected a total of 130 spot speed samples and determined that the variance is 25 (mi/h)2. Has the engineer met all of the requirements of the study?
4-4 一位工程师希望在城市外环路上获得速度特性，并设置95%的置信水平和±1.0 mi/h的允许误差，她收集了130个点速度样本，并确定方差为25 (mi/h)2。工程师是否满足了研究的所有要求？
Known Conditions:
Confidence level: 95% (z = 1.96)
Acceptable error limit: ±1.0 mi/h
Collected samples: 130
Variance: 25 (mi/h)^2 → Standard deviation (σ) = 5 mi/h
Formulation:
N = [(z·σ)/d]^2
Steps:
Calculate required minimum sample size:
N = [1.96(5)/1]^2 = 96.04 ≈ 97 samples
Compare with actual samples collected (130)
Since 130 > 97, the sample size is adequate

[m]4-5 An engineer wishing to determine whether there is a statistically significant difference between the average speed of passenger cars and large trucks on a section of highway collected the data shown below. Determine whether the engineer can conclude that the average speed of large trucks is the same as that for passenger cars.
	Trucks	Passenger Cars
Average Speed mi/h	62 	59
Standard Deviation ±mi/h	5.5	6.3
Sample Size	275	175
4-5 一名工程师希望确定乘用车与大型卡车在一段公路上的平均速度是否有显著性差异。根据以下数据，确定工程师是否能得出结论，即大型卡车的平均速度与乘用车相同。
	卡车	乘用车
平均速度 mi/h	62	59
速度标准差 mi/h	5.5	6.3
样本量	275	175

Known Conditions:
Data for trucks: Average speed = 62 mi/h, Standard deviation = 5.5 mi/h, Sample size = 275
Data for passenger cars: Average speed = 59 mi/h, Standard deviation = 6.3 mi/h, Sample size = 175
Confidence level: 95% (z = 1.96)
Formulation:
Pooled standard deviation: Sd = √[(S1^2/n1) + (S2^2/n2)]
Test statistic: |μ1 - μ2| > z·Sd indicates significant difference
Steps:
Calculate pooled standard deviation:
Sd = √[(5.5^2/275) + (6.3^2/175)] = √(0.11 + 0.2268) = √0.3368 ≈ 0.58
Calculate critical value:
Critical value = 1.96 × 0.58 = 1.137
Calculate absolute difference in means:
|62 - 59| = 3
Compare difference with critical value:
3 > 1.137, therefore the difference is statistically significant

[m]4-18 Data collected at a parking lot indicate that a total of 300 cars park between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m.:
10% of these cars are parked for an average of 2 hr;
30% for an average of 4 hr;
The remaining cars are parked for an average of 10 hr;
Determine the space-hours of demand at the lot.
4-18 在一个停车场收集的数据表明，从早上8点到下午6点共有300辆车停放：10%的车辆平均停放2小时，30%的车辆平均停放4小时，其余车辆的平均停放时间为10小时。计算停车场的空间需求小时数。
公式: 停车场需求的“空间小时数” D=Σ(车辆比例*总车辆数*平均停放时间)

Known Conditions:
Total cars: 300
Parking durations:
10% park for average of 2 hours
30% park for average of 4 hours
60% park for average of 10 hours
Formulation:
D = Σ(percentage of cars × total cars × average parking time)
Steps:
Calculate space-hours for each category:
D = (0.10×300×2) + (0.30×300×4) + (0.60×300×10)
D = 60 + 360 + 1800 = 2,220 space-hours

Demand: 2,220 space-hours (from Problem 4-18)
Vacancy rate: 10%
Efficiency factor: 0.85
Parking period: 10 hours (8 a.m. to 6 p.m.)
Formulation:
N = [D×(1+vacancy rate)]/[E×parking period]
