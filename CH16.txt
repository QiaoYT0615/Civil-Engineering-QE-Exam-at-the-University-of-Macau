[m]16-20 Determine the ground-water infiltration rate for a new two-lane pavement with the following characteristics:
Lane width: 12 feet
Shoulder width: 10 feet
Length of contributing transverse cracks (Wc): 20 feet
Rate of infiltration (Kp): 0.05 cubic feet per day per square foot
Spacing of transverse cracks: 30 feet
16-20 计算新双车道路面的地下水入渗率，其特性如下：
车道宽度：12英尺; 路肩宽度：10英尺; 横向裂缝长度 (Wc)：20英尺; 入渗率 (Kp)：0.05立方英尺/天/平方英尺; 横向裂缝间距：30英尺

Known Conditions:
Two-lane pavement
Lane width: 12 feet
Shoulder width: 10 feet
Length of contributing transverse cracks (Wc): 20 feet
Rate of infiltration (Kp): 0.05 cubic feet per day per square foot
Spacing of transverse cracks: 30 feet

Steps and Equations:
(1) Calculate granular base width:
W = (number of lanes × lane width) + (shoulder width × 2)
W = (2 × 12) + (10 × 2) = 24 + 20 = 44 ft
(2) Determine number of cracks for new pavement:
Nc = N + 1 = 2 + 1 = 3
(3) Calculate infiltration rate using Equation 16.24 (assuming Ic = 2.4):
qi = Ic × (Nc/W + Wc/(W × Sc)) + Kp
qi = 2.4 × (3/44 + 20/(44 × 30)) + 0.05
qi = 2.4 × (0.0682 + 0.0152) + 0.05
qi = 2.4 × 0.0834 + 0.05 = 0.25 ft^3/day/ft^2
