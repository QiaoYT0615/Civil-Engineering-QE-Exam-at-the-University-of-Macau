[m]13-9 A highway project is expected to cost $1,500,000 initially. Annual operating and maintenance cost after the first year is $2000 and will increase by $250 each year for the next 10 years. At the end of the fifth year, the project must be resurfaced at a cost of $300,000.
(a) Calculate the present worth of costs for this project if the annual interest rate is 8%.
(b) Convert the value obtained in (a) to equivalent uniform annual costs.
13-9 一个公路项目预计初期成本为$1,500,000。第一年后的年度运营和维护成本为$2000，并将在接下来的10年里每年增加$250。在第五年结束时，该项目必须以$300,000的成本重新铺设。(a) 如果年利率为8%，计算该项目的成本现值。(b) 将(a)中获得的值转换为等效的年均成本。
公式: 现值公式: PW=初始成本+Σ[C_n/((1+i)^n)]+成本重新铺设
将结果转换为等效年成本(EUAC): EUAC=PW*(A/P,i,n)
(A/P)系数通过公式: A/P=(i(1+i)^n)/[((1+i)^n)-1]
答案: PW=1500000+2000/(1+0.08)^1+2250/(1+0.08)^2+2500/(1+0.08)^3+2750/(1+0.08)^4+...+4500/(1+0.08)^11+300000/(1+0.08)^5=1726020
The present worth (cost) for this project is $1726020.
n=11
EUAC=PW(A/P)=1726020[0.08(1+0.08)^11]/[(1+0.08)^11-1]=241775
The EUAC for this project will be $241775.

The following table shows the initial construction costs, annual operating costs, useful life of the sheeting, and the salvage values for each alternative. Assume that the discount rate is 10%. 公式: PW=初始成本+年运营成本*(P/A,i,n)-残值*(P/F,i,n)
年金现值因子: (P/A,i,n)=[(1+i)^n-1]/[i(1+i)^n]
(P/A,10%,10)=[(1+0.10)^10-1]/[0.10*(1+0.10)^10]=6.145
单笔终值的现值因子，也称为现值系数: (P/F,i,n)=1/(1+i)^n
(P/F,10%,10)=1/(1+0.10)^10=0.3855

Given:
Three alternatives with different:
Initial construction costs
Annual operating costs
Useful life of materials
Salvage values
Discount rate = 10%

Steps and Formulas:
(1) Calculate NPW for Alternative I:
PW1 = Initial cost + Annual cost × (P/A) - Salvage × (P/F)
PW1 = $10,000 + $2,000 × (P/A, 10%, 10) - $2,500 × (P/F, 10%, 10)
PW1 = $10,000 + $2,000 × 6.145 - $2,500 × 0.3855
PW1 = $10,000 + $12,290 - $963.75
PW1 = $21,326
(2) Calculate NPW for Alternative II:
PW2 = $12,000 + $1,600 × 6.145 - $3,000 × 0.3855
PW2 = $12,000 + $9,832 - $1,156.5
PW2 = $20,676
(3) Calculate NPW for Alternative III (need to adjust for different life cycle):
PW3 = $5,000 + $2,500 × 6.145 + ($5,000 + $500) × (P/F, 10%, 5) + $500 × (P/F, 10%, 10)
PW3 = $5,000 + $15,362.5 + $5,500 × 0.6209 + $500 × 0.3855
PW3 = $5,000 + $15,362.5 + $3,415 + $193
PW3 = $22,964
Result: Alternative II has lowest NPW ($20,676) and is preferred.

6.交通运营评估

[m]13-20 A new carpool lane has replaced one lane of an existing six-lane highway. During peak hours, the lane is restricted to cars carrying three or more passengers. After five months of operation, the carpool lane handles 800 autos/h, whereas the existing lanes are operating at capacity levels of 1500 veh/h/ln at an occupancy rate of 1.2. How would you determine if the new carpool lane is successful or if the lane should be open to all traffic?
13-20 一条新拼车车道取代了现有六车道高速公路中的一条车道。在高峰时段，该车道仅限三人及以上的车辆通行。运行五个月后，拼车车道处理800辆车/小时，而现有车道以1.2的乘员率达到1500辆车/小时/车道的容量。如何判断此新拼车车道是否成功，或者该车道是否应向所有车辆开放？

Given:
New carpool lane handles 800 autos/h with 3+ passengers
Existing lanes operate at 1500 veh/h/ln with 1.2 occupancy rate
5 months of operation

Steps and Formulas:
(1) Calculate person throughput for carpool lane:
Person throughput = Vehicle volume × Average occupancy
Person throughput = 800 × 3 = 2,400 persons/hour
(2) Calculate person throughput for regular lane considering growth:
Person throughput = Vehicle volume × Average occupancy × (1 + growth rate)
Person throughput = 1,500 × 1.2 × (1 + 0.04) = 1,872 persons/hour
(3) Compare:
2,400 > 1,872
Result: Carpool lane is successful as it carries more people per hour than a regular lane would, even accounting for traffic growth.
