[m]8.4.4 应用类沉降问题 Field Settlement Estimation & Engineering Application

公式 1: 常固结土沉降公式 / Settlement in Normally Consolidated Clay
适用于OCR = 1的土层，沉降由主压缩主导。
For NC clay (OCR = 1), settlement is governed by virgin compression.
sc = Ccε * H_0 * log((σ′vo + Δσv) / σ′vo)
(Ccε = modified compression index; H_0 = clay layer thickness [m or ft]; σ′vo = initial vertical effective stress [kPa or psf]; Δσv = stress increase [kPa or psf])

公式 2: 超固结土沉降（两段式）/ Settlement in Overconsolidated Clay (Two-Part Formula)
用于OCR > 1的土体，沉降由回压缩与主压缩两段组成。
For OC clay, settlement includes recompression and virgin compression phases.
sc = Crε * H_0 * log(σ′p / σ′vo) + Ccε * H_0 * log((σ′vo + Δσv) / σ′p)
(Crε = modified recompression index; σ′p = preconsolidation pressure)

公式 3: 卸载引起的膨胀 / Swell Due to Unloading
当外荷移除时土层会回弹膨胀。
Upon unloading, swelling occurs due to stress release.
s_swell = Crε * H_0 * log(σ′before / σ′after)
(σ′before = effective stress before unloading; σ′after = after unloading)

公式 4: 双折线压缩曲线计算 / Bilinear Compression Curve
适用于存在两个不同斜率的e–logσ′段落。
Used when compression curve has two slopes.
εv = C_1 * log(σ′2 / σ′1) + C_2 * log(σ′3 / σ′2)
(C_1, C_2 = slope of each segment; σ′ = effective stress)
sc = εv * H_0
(εv = total vertical strain; H_0 = layer thickness)

公式 5: 压缩曲线估算 Cr 与 Cc / Estimating Cr and Cc from Compression Curve
根据e–logσ′图中不同区段的斜率求得。
Derived from slopes of e–logσ′ plot.
Cc = (e_1 - e_2) / log(σ′2 / σ′1) (Virgin 压缩段）
Cr = (e_1 - e_2) / log(σ′2 / σ′1) (回压缩段）
(e = void ratio; σ′ = effective vertical stress)

公式 6: 使用应变计算沉降 / Settlement from Vertical Strain
由已知应变计算沉降。
Compute settlement from vertical strain.
sc = ε * H_0
(ε = vertical strain; H_0 = layer thickness)

公式 7: 对数插值法 / Logarithmic Interpolation
在半对数坐标中估算中间应力。
Estimate unknown stress along log scale.
x = (x_2)^f * (x_1)^(1-f), f = a / (a + b)
(x_1, x_2 = known stress points; f = interpolation factor)

单位与换算参考 / Units & Conversions:
1 ft = 0.3048 m
1 psf = 47.88 Pa
1 kPa = 1 kN/m^2
log 表达式 = 常用以10为底 log_10
e, Cc, Cr, ε = dimensionless (无单位)
Ccε, Crε = 修正指数，常用于图像回归

[m]Q8-31. Figure P8.31 shows a proposed foundation site, with 10 ft of sand overlying 15 ft of clay with consolidation properties shown. The clay is normally consolidated. Assume 1-D conditions. 
(a) Compute the initial σ'v at the middle of the clay layer prior to excavation and construction. 
(b) After excavation and during construction, the foundation area will be heavily loaded with the structure and equipment so that σ'v at the middle of the clay layer will be increased to 3900 psf. Determine the settlement that will occur under these conditions. 
(c) After construction is completed, the equipment will be removed and the final σ'v at the middle of the clay layer will be 3200 psf. 

[m]SOLUTION: 
Normal consolidation --- OCR=1 σ'p=σ'v0
Eq. σ'v0 = (γ_d*H)_Sand+(γ_d*H)_Clay = (γ_d*H)_Sand+((γt-γw)*H)_Clay
(a) σ'v0 = (10 ft)(110 pcf) + (7.5 ft)(120 - 62.4 pcf) = 1532 psf
(b) σ'v0+Δσv = 3900 psf
Δσv = (σ'v0+Δσv)-σ'v0 = 3900 - 1532 = 2368 psf
Now (σ'v0+Δσv)＞σ'p, normal consolidation --- using Ccε.
Given: Ccε = 0.165, Crε = 0.033, H0 = 15 ft
Eq. 8.13: sc = Ccε * H0 * log((σ'v0 + Δσv) / σ'v0)
sc = (0.165)(15 ft) * log((1532 + 2368) / 1532) = 1.0 ft
(c) Δσv = 3200 - 1532 = 1668 psf
3200 psf ＜ 3900 psf 历史最大超过现有 --- 回弹 swell
Compute the swell that occurs after equipment is removed (part b to part c):
s_swell = Crε * H0 * log(σb - σc)
s_swell = (0.033)(15) * (log(3900) - log(3200)) = 0.0425 ft
The final settlement = 1.0 - 0.0425 = 0.96 ft

[m]Q8-32. As part of a construction project, a 7.5 m thick layer of clay is to be loaded with a temporary 3 m thick sand layer (refer to Fig. P8.32). The figure shows the water-table location, soil unit weights, and the compression curve properties for the clay. Assume the sand layer remains dry.
(a) Calculate the value of σ'v in the middle of the clay layer (at 3.75 m below the water table) before the sand layer is applied, and after consolidation is complete.
(b) Based on your answer in part (a), and the compression curve characteristics, calculate the settlement that will occur under these conditions.
(c) How much will the clay layer heave when the 3 m sand layer is removed?

[m]Solution:
(a) To calculate the value of σ'v before the sand layer is applied:
σ'v0 = (γ_d*H)_Clay = ((γt-γw)*H)_Clay
σ'v0 = (3.75 m) × (20.5 - 9.81 kN/m^3) = 40.09 kN/m^2
(b) For the settlement calculation, first, find Δσv:
Δσv = (3 m) × (16 kN/m^3) = 48.0 kN/m^2
Next, calculate the OCR (Over Consolidation Ratio):
OCR = σ'p / σ'vo = 74 kPa / 40.09 kPa = 1.85＞1
σ'vo+Δσv＞σ'p --- consider Crε+Ccε
Now, use the formula from Eq. 8.19b to calculate the settlement sc:
sc = Crε H0 log(σ'p / σ'vo) + Ccε H0 log((σ'v0 + Δσv) / σ'p)
Substituting the values:
sc = (0.03)(7.5 m) log(74 / 40.09) + (0.18)(7.5 m) log((40.09 + 48) / 74)

(c) To calculate the heave when the sand layer is removed:
removed之后就剩 σ'v=σ'vo＜σ'vo+Δσv --- consider Csε
s_swell = Csε Ho log(σ'vf / σ'vo)
s_swell = (0.03)(7.5 m) log((40.09 + 48.0) / 40.09) = (7.5 m)*(0.01026)

If the final σ'v at the middle of the layer after the embankment loading is 2100 psf, what is the settlement, in inches, of the clay layer resulting from this loading?

sc = 0.3031 + 0.1808
sc = 0.4839 ft = 5.8 inches
Thus, the settlement is 5.8 inches.

[m]8.3.3 密度与饱和度计算

公式 10 [ρ_d - 干密度 Dry Density]
Meaning: Dry density is the mass of solids divided by total volume.
ρ_d = Ms / Vt 
(Ms: dry mass in grams; Vt: total volume in cm^3)

公式 11 [ρ_t - 湿密度 Wet Density]
Meaning: Wet density is total mass divided by total volume.
ρ_t = M_total / Vt 
(M_total: total mass in grams)

公式 12 [S - 饱和度 Degree of Saturation]
Meaning: S is the ratio of water volume to void volume.
S = (Vw / Vv) × 100 
(Vw: volume of water; Vv: volume of voids in cm^3)

公式 13 [S from w and e]
Meaning: S estimated using water content and void ratio.
S = (ρs × w) / (ρw × e) × 100
(ρs: solid density in Mg/m^3; w: water content in decimal; ρw: water density = 1.0 Mg/m^3; e: void ratio)

Unit Conversion Reference: 1 Mg/m^3 = 1000 kg/m^3 = 1 g/cm^3

[m]8.4.3 超固结压力与沉降分析 Preconsolidation Pressure and Settlement Analysis

公式 4：[修正压缩指数定义式 Modified Compression Index from Strain-Pressure Data]
[公式含义]Ccε 表示应变–logσ' 曲线中的斜率。
[Meaning]Ccε represents the slope of the strain–logσ' curve.
Ccε = (ε2 - ε1) / log10(σ'2 / σ'1)
(ε：百分应变，单位 %；σ'：有效应力，单位 kPa)

公式 5：[饱和单位重计算 Saturated Unit Weight Estimate]
[公式含义]用于估算饱和条件下土的单位重。
[Meaning]Estimation of saturated unit weight from dry density and water content.
ρ_sat = ρ_dry x (1 + w)
(ρ_dry：干密度[Mg/m^3]；w：含水率，小数)

公式 6：[有效应力估算 Estimate Vertical Effective Stress at Mid-Layer]
[公式含义]整合填土和饱和土层单位重估算总有效应力。
[Meaning]Combined vertical effective stress from fill and underlying saturated clay.
σ'v = [Σ(γ_i x h_i)] x g
(γ_i：单位重[Mg/m^3]；h_i：厚度[m]；g = 9.81 m/s^2)
Δσv 通常为填土引起的荷载 = γ_fill x h_fill x g

[m]8.1 压缩指数与e-logσ'关系 Compression Index and e-logσ' Relationship

公式 1：[压缩指数定义式]
公式含义：压缩指数（Cc）描述孔隙比e随对数有效应力σ'变化的速率。
Meaning: The compression index (Cc) expresses the slope of the e–logσ' curve.
Cc = (e1 - e2) / log(σ'2 / σ'1)
（Cc：压缩指数，无单位；e：孔隙比；σ'：有效应力[kPa]）

公式 2：[经验拟合曲线表达式]
公式含义：e-logσ'关系可近似为线性对数关系，用以描述压缩过程。
Meaning: The e–logσ' curve can be empirically fitted as a linear function.

[m]8.4 沉降计算方法 Settlement Calculation Methods

8.4.1 粘土层沉降计算 Settlement in Clay Layers

公式 1：[OC土的分段沉降公式（两段式）Settlement in Overconsolidated Clay (Two-Stage)]
[公式含义]对于超固结土，沉降由两部分组成：预压密段与主压缩段。
[Meaning]For OC clays, settlement is calculated using rebound and virgin compression stages.
sc = Cr x H0 x log10(σ'p / σ'vo) + Cc x H0 x log10((σ'vo + Δσv) / σ'p)

[公式含义]对于NC土，全段为 virgin 压缩，沉降取决于应力增量。
[Meaning]For NC clays, settlement is fully from virgin compression.
sc = Cc x H0 x log10((σ'vo + Δσv) / σ'vo)
(Cc：压缩指数，其它同上)

Given:
- H0 = 7.5 m, e1 = 1.9
- From the plot: p1 = 170 kPa, p2 = 2 * p1 = 340 kPa --  e2 = 1.49 (from the plot)
Using Eq. 8.18b to compute the settlement:
sc = H0(ε) = H0 * (Δe / (1 + e0)) = (7.5 m) * (1.9 - 1.49) / (1 + 1.9)
sc = 1.06 m = 106 mm
